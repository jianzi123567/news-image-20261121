bounty **news-image-20261121** ("Sum up 21 November's world news in one
image", $100, 50 winners).

## The image

A newspaper-style infographic, 1024×1024, with five panels under the
headline "WORLD NEWS — 21 NOVEMBER 2026":

1. **MISSILES LAND IN POLAND** — Russian cruise missiles crash in
   south-east Poland near allied war games; Warsaw calls it a deliberate
   provocation and NATO jets are scrambled. (The Times / AP)
2. **RIYADH INTERCEPTS THREE MISSILES** — Saudi defences shoot down three
   Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh, the capital's third night
   of air-raid alerts. (Reuters)
3. **UKRAINE PEACE TALKS ADVANCE** — US envoys report progress on a peace
   framework, with NATO-style security guarantees on the table. (RFE/RL)
4. **900,000 AI CHIPS FOR CHINA** — The US approved nearly 900,000 Nvidia
   and AMD AI chips for Chinese firms in six months, House committee
   records show. (Reuters)
5. **INDONESIA PRESSES GOOGLE** — Jakarta demands Google explain removing
   more than 19,000 YouTube channels supportive of the government.
   (Reuters)

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3) reads the bytes of `entry.png` (shipped in the
repo root), checks the file is a JPEG or PNG under 1 MiB, and writes
those exact bytes to `/output/entry.png`, verifying the copy afterwards.
No network, no randomness, no time, no environment — two runs produce
identical bytes.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB, JPEG bytes)
- `main.py` — the program that writes `/output/entry.png`
